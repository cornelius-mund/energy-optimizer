Household load

Solid lines are prices from published day-ahead prices. The dashed line is the EPEX Predictor forecast for the hours after the last published price (prices CC-BY-4.0 energy-charts.info and ENTSO-E, weather data Open-Meteo.com). Prices are the ones the plan uses: with a configured tariff, import prices include grid fees, levies and VAT and export prices are the feed-in remuneration; without one, both are market prices.
